Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visiting FitBalance

What is included in the trial session and price?

The trial session includes full access to the gym area, locker rooms, showers, and an introductory tour with advice on our membership options.

Can I pause or freeze my membership?

Yes, standard memberships can be frozen for 7 to 30 days depending on your subscription plan in case of travel or illness.

Is a medical certificate required for pool access?

Yes, a valid medical health certificate is required for aqua zone access according to standard facility safety rules.
